Leadership & Educational Vision

Meet the Founder — Ahmed Ramadan, MBA

Ahmed Ramadan | NeoClinIQ Founder

Pharmacy Education, Clinical Practice & Healthcare Leadership

Ahmed Ramadan is an internationally trained pharmacy professional, healthcare educator and founder of NeoClinIQ.

His background combines pharmacy education, clinical practice experience, pharmaceutical business development and healthcare leadership.

A graduate of the University of Toronto's International Pharmacy Graduate program, he served as a class representative and was selected as an IPG Alumni Ambassador.

Through NeoClinIQ, he develops structured, visually focused educational resources designed to make clinical therapeutics more accessible to pharmacy learners.
