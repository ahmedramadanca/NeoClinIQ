ABOUT NEOCLINIQ

A visual approach to Canadian clinical education

NeoClinIQ is a Canadian healthcare education initiative designed to make complex pharmacy topics easier to understand, review and apply.

Our resources support internationally educated pharmacists, pharmacy learners and healthcare professionals through structured clinical review, therapeutic decision-making and practical communication skills.

NeoClinIQ is an initiative of Maple Canada Ramadan .

PEBC QUALIFYING EXAMINATION — PART I

MCQ preparation grounded in clinical reasoning

NeoClinIQ's MCQ learning approach connects therapeutic knowledge with patient cases, evidence-based medication choices and the practical application of pharmacy concepts.

Therapeutics review

Structured chapters across major clinical systems, from infectious diseases and cardiovascular care to endocrinology and mental health.

Applied questions

Practice clinical decision-making through MCQ-style review, case interpretation and answer discussion.

Visual reinforcement

Use infographics and concise summaries to reinforce key concepts and revisit challenging subjects.

For current exam requirements, blueprints and sample questions, consult the official PEBC website .

PEBC QUALIFYING EXAMINATION — PART II

OSCE preparation for patient-centred practice

Build confidence in clinical encounters by connecting knowledge with assessment, communication, safety and clear recommendations.

Patient assessment

Gather relevant information, identify drug therapy problems and prioritize patient needs.

Counselling and communication

Practice explaining treatments, checking understanding and communicating with patients and healthcare professionals.

Station-style practice

Develop a structured approach to time-limited clinical scenarios, therapeutic decisions and professional interactions.
