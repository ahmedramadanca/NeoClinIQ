ABOUT NEOCLINIQ

A visual approach to clinical education.

NeoClinIQ is a Canadian healthcare education initiative focused on organizing complex clinical information into clear, structured and practical learning resources.

The platform is designed for pharmacy learners, internationally educated pharmacists and healthcare professionals seeking efficient, clinically relevant review.

NeoClinIQ supports the development of clinical knowledge, therapeutic reasoning and examination preparation through organized educational content and visual learning.
