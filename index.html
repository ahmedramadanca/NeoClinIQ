Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

NeoClinIQ is operated by 15095271 Canada Inc., operating as Maple Canada Ramadan (“Maple Canada Ramadan,” “NeoClinIQ,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling personal information responsibly and transparently. This Privacy Policy explains what information NeoClinIQ may collect, why it is collected, how it is used, how it may be shared with service providers, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address
  • User account identifier
  • Authentication and login information
  • Account status

Subscription and purchase information

  • Subscription status
  • Product or subscription plan
  • Purchase or entitlement status
  • Subscription expiry or renewal information
  • Store-generated purchase identifiers

Payments are processed by the applicable app marketplace, such as Apple. NeoClinIQ does not directly receive or store your complete payment-card information. NeoClinIQ uses RevenueCat to help manage subscription entitlements and purchase status.

Learning and application activity

  • Learning progress
  • Chapter activity
  • Saved learning preferences
  • Bookmarks
  • Application settings
  • Other educational activity associated with the app

Some learning information may be stored locally on your device.

Technical information

Our service providers may process limited technical information required to operate, secure and troubleshoot NeoClinIQ, such as device or application identifiers, authentication information, network or server request information, and error or operational information.

2. How We Use Information

  • Creating and authenticating user accounts
  • Providing access to educational content
  • Confirming subscription entitlements
  • Restoring eligible purchases
  • Maintaining user learning functionality
  • Protecting the security and integrity of the service
  • Troubleshooting technical problems
  • Responding to support requests
  • Meeting legal and regulatory obligations
  • Improving the reliability and functionality of NeoClinIQ

We do not sell personal information.

3. Service Providers

Supabase — used for application infrastructure, authentication, database services and backend functionality.

RevenueCat — used to manage subscription entitlement information and purchase status.

Apple — where NeoClinIQ is distributed through the Apple App Store, Apple processes purchases, subscriptions and associated App Store transactions according to Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

Google Drive / Google Services — NeoClinIQ may use Google services as part of its internal content-storage and content-delivery infrastructure. Ordinary NeoClinIQ users are not granting NeoClinIQ access to their personal Google Drive accounts. Access to administrative content infrastructure is controlled by NeoClinIQ.

We expect service providers processing personal information on our behalf to apply appropriate safeguards consistent with their contractual and legal obligations.

4. Payments

NeoClinIQ does not directly process complete payment-card information for App Store purchases. Subscription billing is handled by the relevant application marketplace.

Users are responsible for managing cancellation or renewal of store subscriptions through the applicable store account. Deleting a NeoClinIQ account does not necessarily cancel an active App Store subscription.

5. Account Deletion

NeoClinIQ provides an in-app method for eligible users to request deletion of their NeoClinIQ account.

When account deletion is completed, NeoClinIQ will delete or de-identify personal information associated with that account that we control, subject to information that must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting or regulatory purposes.

Deleting your NeoClinIQ account does not automatically cancel a subscription billed through Apple or another application marketplace. Subscriptions must be managed separately through the relevant store.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or as otherwise required or permitted by law.

  • The type of information
  • Whether an account remains active
  • Subscription or transaction requirements
  • Security and fraud-prevention requirements
  • Legal, regulatory or accounting obligations
  • The need to resolve disputes or enforce agreements

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, anonymize or securely dispose of it.

7. Security

NeoClinIQ uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration or loss.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security, but we aim to use safeguards appropriate to the nature and sensitivity of the information being processed.

8. International Processing

Some service providers supporting NeoClinIQ may process or store information outside your province or outside Canada. When information is processed in another jurisdiction, it may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction and may be accessible to government, regulatory or law-enforcement authorities where legally permitted.

9. Your Privacy Rights

  • Information about personal information we hold about you
  • Access to personal information associated with your account
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of eligible personal information
  • Information about how your personal information is used
  • Information regarding relevant disclosures or service providers

Requests may be submitted using the contact information below. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

10. Consent and Withdrawal

Where consent is the appropriate legal basis for processing personal information, you may withdraw consent subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice. Withdrawal of consent may affect our ability to provide features that require the relevant information.

11. Children's Privacy

NeoClinIQ is designed primarily for pharmacy learners, healthcare professionals and adult users pursuing clinical education. NeoClinIQ is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from young children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to NeoClinIQ inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

12. Educational Purpose

NeoClinIQ provides educational information and clinical learning resources. NeoClinIQ is not a substitute for professional clinical judgment, individualized medical assessment, diagnosis or treatment.

Users remain responsible for consulting appropriate professional standards, current clinical guidelines, product information and applicable laws and regulations when making clinical decisions.

Users should not submit identifiable patient information or confidential patient health information to NeoClinIQ unless a feature expressly permits and appropriately protects such information.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in NeoClinIQ, applicable law, technology or privacy practices. When material changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
