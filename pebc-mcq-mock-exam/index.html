What makes the mock different?

Use the question bank for regular practice by topic. The mock groups questions into one timed sitting so learners can practise pacing and see which topics need more review. All 200 questions count toward the NeoClinIQ practice score.

NeoClinIQ is not affiliated with or endorsed by PEBC. This is an independent practice tool, not an official PEBC exam or a prediction of your exam result.

Prepare for a meaningful timed practice sitting

Set aside uninterrupted time before starting the mock. Read each question carefully, identify the patient details relevant to the decision and avoid spending too long on one uncertain item. The aim is to identify strengths and study gaps under time pressure, not to estimate an official PEBC score.

After finishing, group incorrect questions by topic and reason: missing clinical information, incorrect pharmacotherapy reasoning, overlooked safety issue or time pressure. Return to the course overview for structured review and the practice-tests guide for a question-review routine. Consult the official PEBC preparation information for current exam requirements.
