Frequently asked questions

Find introductory answers about NeoClinIQ programs and access.

How do I access the SI OSCE Tutor?

The NeoClinIQ SI OSCE Tutor is included with an active NeoClinIQ OSCE subscription. Sign in through the Learning Portal and select Open SI OSCE Tutor.

What is the 96-Day Progressive Mastery plan?

The planners organize preparation across 16 weeks. Active subscribers can access their corresponding planner through the Learning Portal.

Can I try NeoClinIQ before enrolling?

Watch the free masterclass and CTC reviews and try the free quiz.

How does SI OSCE timing work?

Mapped practice stations have two minutes for door notes and seven minutes for the encounter. The clock pauses while SI prepares its reply, so you never lose encounter time. The timed clinical update may interrupt the candidate.

Where can I find official exam dates?

Consult the official PEBC examination dates and fees.

What is the difference between Simulator, Ultra and Ultimate?

Simulator (C$700) is exam practice only: the 5,000-question bank and 21 timed mock exams. Ultra (C$1,300) is study only: SI Super Intelligence, the 14-module library and the 96-Day MCQ Mastery Plan. Ultimate (C$1,800) includes everything in both.

How long do I get access?

Each website bundle includes four calendar months of access, starting when your enrolment is complete.

Can I extend my plan?

Yes. Each plan offers one optional four-month extension on the same tier at half the initial price. You can buy it before your access ends or within 42 days after expiry; the grace period is a purchase window and does not itself grant access.

Is the OSCE program included in the MCQ bundles?

No. The OSCE program (C$1,800), including the SI OSCE Tutor, is a separate enrolment from the MCQ website bundles and from mobile-app subscriptions.

How do I enrol and pay?

Sign in to the Learning Portal and complete checkout there. Your access opens as soon as enrolment is complete. Questions before you enrol: email neocliniq@maplecanadaramadan.ca.
