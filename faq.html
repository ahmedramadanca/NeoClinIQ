Is NeoClinIQ affiliated with PEBC?

No. NeoClinIQ is an independent educational resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Pharmacy Examining Board of Canada.

Does NeoClinIQ guarantee a pass?

No educational program can guarantee an examination result. Candidates should consult PEBC for official eligibility, competencies and examination rules.
