What happens in a practice station?

The steps below apply to the Practice Case Library. The separate scripted sample is a preview with a self-assessment checklist, not AI roleplay or transcript-based assessment.

  1. Choose the MCQ or OSCE case library, then a station category, module, chapter and case.
  2. Read a two-minute door note, then begin a seven-minute encounter. The clock pauses while SI prepares a reply or timed update.
  3. Speak or type to a simulated patient or prescriber. Open the fictional patient pharmacy profile where the station provides one.
  4. Review transcript-based feedback, a case-specific 15-point rubric and areas to improve.

OSCE station categories

  • Minor ailments
  • OTC recommendations
  • Natural product inquiries
  • Prescription dispensing
  • Prescription counselling
  • Healthcare-provider inquiries
  • Patient prescription inquiries
  • Prescription validation
  • Referral scope

NeoClinIQ is independent of PEBC. Tutor scores are formative practice feedback, built from the NeoClinIQ clinical library and Canadian references.

How to reflect after a practice station

During an encounter, prioritize establishing the patient's concern, asking focused questions and identifying situations requiring immediate referral or additional information. In the door-note period, identify the task, role and information you must confirm rather than memorizing an entire response.

After the practice, compare your actual dialogue with the station-specific formative checklist. Note whether you gathered essential facts, recognized safety concerns, explained your recommendation in plain language and checked the other person's understanding. Select one or two missed actions to practise in the next station.

For a broader preparation sequence, see the PEBC OSCE preparation guide and public MCQ and OSCE study guide. For official competencies and current examination instructions, consult the Pharmacy Examining Board of Canada.
