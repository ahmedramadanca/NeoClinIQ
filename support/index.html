Support

Help with NeoClinIQ accounts, subscriptions, learning access, purchases and technical issues.

Contact NeoClinIQ Support

For technical assistance, account questions, subscription support, content-access issues or general NeoClinIQ inquiries, contact:

NeoClinIQ Support

Account Support

NeoClinIQ users can create and manage their account within the application. If you experience difficulty signing in or accessing your account, contact NeoClinIQ Support using the email above.

Subscription Support

NeoClinIQ subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store are billed and managed by Apple.

NeoClinIQ can verify subscription access and entitlements, while billing, cancellation and refund decisions for App Store purchases are generally managed through Apple.

If you previously purchased an eligible NeoClinIQ subscription, use the Restore Purchases option inside the app.

Managing or Cancelling an Apple Subscription

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your Apple Account name.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select NeoClinIQ.
  5. Review, change or cancel the subscription as available.

Account Deletion

NeoClinIQ provides an in-app account deletion option. Deleting your account removes eligible account data controlled by NeoClinIQ, subject to applicable legal and operational retention requirements.

Important: deleting your NeoClinIQ account does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store subscription. Store subscriptions must be managed separately.

Learning Content Access

Some NeoClinIQ learning resources require an active subscription. If your subscription is active but premium content is unavailable, try restoring purchases and confirm that you are signed in to the NeoClinIQ account associated with your access.

If the issue continues, contact support and include:

  • The email address associated with your NeoClinIQ account
  • Your device type
  • A brief description of the problem
  • A screenshot of any error message, if available

Please do not include passwords, payment-card information, or identifiable patient information in support messages.

Educational Use

NeoClinIQ provides educational and clinical learning resources. It does not provide emergency medical services, individualized diagnosis, treatment or patient-specific medical advice.
