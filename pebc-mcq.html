Your four-month / 16-week pathway

Month 1 · Weeks 1–4

Foundations

Review official PEBC candidate guidance, establish your baseline and begin systematic therapeutic review.

Month 2 · Weeks 5–8

Clinical application

Deepen clinical knowledge through case-based questions and structured error review.

Month 3 · Weeks 9–12

Integrated practice

Mix therapeutic systems, strengthen time management and revisit recurring knowledge gaps.

Month 4 · Weeks 13–16

Exam readiness

Consolidate high-yield concepts, practise under timed conditions and refine your revision plan.

This is an illustrative preparation framework, not an official PEBC study schedule or a promise of exam readiness.

Frequently asked questions

Is NeoClinIQ affiliated with PEBC?

No. NeoClinIQ is an independent educational platform and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Pharmacy Examining Board of Canada.

Does the course guarantee a passing result?

No. Examination outcomes depend on individual preparation and performance. NeoClinIQ does not guarantee results.

Is the four-month plan the official PEBC schedule?

No. It is NeoClinIQ's illustrative 16-week preparation framework. Consult PEBC for official examination dates, eligibility and candidate information.

Are all app features and questions immediately available?

Content and features may vary by course and development stage. Confirm current access, supported devices and included resources before enrolling.

How can I enrol?

Contact neocliniq@maplecanadaramadan.ca for questions about what’s included or mentorship arrangements. Sign in to the Learning Portal to purchase website access.

Official examination resources

For the current PEBC examination blueprint, eligibility, policies and examination dates, refer directly to the Pharmacy Examining Board of Canada. NeoClinIQ provides independent preparation resources, not official PEBC exam questions.
