NeoClinIQ MCQ preparation

NeoClinIQ supports internationally educated and Canadian pharmacy learners preparing for the PEBC Pharmacist Qualifying Examination Part I. Study clinical decision-making with organized review materials and practice-oriented learning.

The MCQ learning approach

Review clinical topics systematically, identify knowledge gaps, practise applying therapeutics principles and revisit challenging concepts. NeoClinIQ materials are educational resources, not official PEBC questions.
