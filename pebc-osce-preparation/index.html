How to Prepare for the PEBC OSCE

Prepare for the PEBC OSCE with structured pharmacy practice, clinical case review and timed simulation through the NeoClinIQ SI OSCE Tutor.

NeoClinIQ is a Canadian visual clinical education initiative supporting internationally educated pharmacists and other candidates preparing for Canadian pharmacy practice and licensing assessments.

Practise a timed OSCE station

Use the About SI OSCE Tutor to rehearse patient and prescriber encounters and get feedback on your dialogue. The tutor is available with an active NeoClinIQ OSCE subscription.

Develop your clinical encounter skills

Gather relevant information

Practise focused questioning, medication history and identifying red flags.

Make a clinical recommendation

Explain your assessment, treatment options, referral decisions and monitoring.

Practise timed encounters

Use the SI OSCE Tutor for simulated clinical conversations.

Build consistency

Follow the 96-Day OSCE Mastery planner and review formative feedback.

After a simulated encounter

Use a consistent review framework: What was the presenting concern? Which patient details or medication information changed the recommendation? Was a red flag missed? Did the candidate explain next steps, safety advice and follow-up in language a patient could use?

Practise communicating with both patients and health-care professionals. A patient-facing consultation may emphasize symptoms, preferences and teach-back, while a prescriber inquiry may need a concise clinical recommendation with supporting rationale. Record which communication step to improve, then repeat the skill in a different case rather than only memorizing one script.

For a complete pathway, review the 16-week OSCE program overview, the SI OSCE Tutor explanation, and the public study guide. Practice station scores are formative; the official examination content and scoring remain the responsibility of PEBC.

PEBC OSCE preparation

The Pharmacist Qualifying Examination Part II (OSCE) assesses applied pharmacy practice through simulated encounters. NeoClinIQ provides independent educational practice.

Consult the official PEBC examination preparation information for current requirements.
