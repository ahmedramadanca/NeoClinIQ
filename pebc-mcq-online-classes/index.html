How to get more from an online review session

Before watching, write down what you already know about the topic and one clinical decision you find difficult. During the explanation, listen for how the patient's presentation, medical conditions, medication history and treatment goals affect the plan. After the session, summarize the key decision points in your own words rather than copying slides.

PEBC exam preparation

The PEBC Pharmacist Qualifying Examination Part I (MCQ) assesses application of knowledge and professional judgment. NeoClinIQ is an independent educational provider, not affiliated with or endorsed by PEBC.

Always consult the official PEBC Part I preparation information and the current examination blueprint.
