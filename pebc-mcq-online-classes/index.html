NeoClinIQ is a Canadian visual clinical education initiative supporting internationally educated pharmacists and other candidates preparing for Canadian pharmacy practice and licensing assessments.

PEBC exam preparation

The PEBC Pharmacist Qualifying Examination Part I (MCQ) assesses application of knowledge and professional judgment. NeoClinIQ is an independent educational provider, not affiliated with or endorsed by PEBC.

Always consult the official PEBC Part I preparation information and the current examination blueprint.
