Access and renewal policy

Initial purchase: MCQ: four calendar months. OSCE: 16 weeks.
Extensions: One discounted extension per enrollment, purchasable before expiry or within 42 days afterward. Both MCQ and OSCE add four calendar months. Access stops at expiry during the grace period. An extension purchased after expiry starts a new extension period on payment.
New cycle: Once access expires, a new full-price initial purchase is permitted and extension eligibility resets. After the 42-day grace period, a discounted extension is no longer available.
Independent access: MCQ and OSCE are separate. Website access does not grant mobile-app subscriptions.
