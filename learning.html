Access and renewal policy

Initial purchase: Each MCQ plan includes four calendar months of access to the features shown. OSCE remains 16 weeks.
New MCQ plan extensions: Eligible learners can buy one additional four-calendar-month term at 50% of the same plan’s initial price: Simulator C$350, Ultra C$650, or Ultimate C$900. Buy before expiry or within 42 days afterward. The grace period does not provide free access. There is no automatic renewal.
OSCE: Existing OSCE terms are unchanged. Eligible OSCE learners may purchase one four-month extension for C$900 before expiry or within 42 days afterward.
Existing MCQ subscribers: You keep the access you already paid for until it expires. Then you can choose from the three current MCQ plans.
Independent access: MCQ and OSCE are separate. Website access does not grant mobile-app subscriptions.
