NeoClinIQ OSCE preparation

The PEBC OSCE assesses applied pharmacy practice skills in simulated scenarios. NeoClinIQ focuses on structured preparation, patient-centred communication and clinical reasoning.

How to prepare

Practise gathering relevant information, identifying drug-therapy problems, explaining recommendations clearly and managing the time available in a station. Use official PEBC information for the current examination format.

Explore OSCE support

Enroll in the full 16-week OSCE preparation program for CAD $1,800. One optional four-calendar-month extension costs CAD $900 and can be purchased before expiry or within 42 days afterward. The grace period does not grant learning access.
