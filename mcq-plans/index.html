Extend your plan once, at half price

Each new MCQ plan offers one optional extension of four calendar months on the same tier, at half the initial price. If eligible, purchase before your access ends or within 42 days after expiry. The grace period is a purchase window only: it does not give you free access.

Access does not renew automatically. If you extend before expiry, four months are added to the current term. If you extend after expiry, the new term begins when Stripe payment is confirmed.

Before you enrol

The Learning Portal is where enrolment and same-tier extensions are completed after you sign in. Existing subscribers keep access they already paid for until expiry.

The plans cover the NeoClinIQ website only. OSCE and mobile-app subscriptions are separate. NeoClinIQ is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by PEBC.
