How to review PEBC-style practice questions

After each question, separate the clinical information from the answer options. Identify the patient's main problem, relevant history, medications, contraindications and treatment goals. Explain why the preferred answer fits the scenario before reading the rationale, then explain why the other choices do not.

Classify missed questions into knowledge, interpretation, clinical reasoning or time-management errors. Revisit the related topic with the study materials overview, and check whether you can solve a similar question later without recalling the original answer. The free HIV, CKD and STIs quiz provides a short demonstration; the timed 200-question mock is a distinct subscriber practice experience.
